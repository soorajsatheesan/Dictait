# The Dictait mark

A speech-bubble **D** with a text caret cut through its bowl: your voice becomes text at the cursor.

- `mark.svg` is the mark in ember (#FF5B36); `mark-mono.svg` is the black template the menu bar icons are made from.
- `app-icon.svg` is the macOS app icon on the Big Sur grid (824 px body in a 1024 canvas), with the logo gradient #FF9456 → #FF4A2E on graphite.

`../icon.png`, `../../build/icon.icns` and `../tray/*.png` are rendered from these files. Keep the mark's path identical everywhere it appears (the island, the sidebar, the website).
